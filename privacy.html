Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

At StateTaxCalc, your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit statetaxcalc.com and use our tax calculation tools.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection:

  • Calculation Data: The numerical inputs (income, deductions) you enter are processed locally in your browser to generate instant results. We do not store this sensitive financial data on our servers.
  • Account Information: If you purchase a Premium Tax Assessment, basic contact information (like your email address) is collected strictly for delivering the product and sending receipts.
  • Analytics: We collect standard, anonymized web analytics data (such as browser type, referring domain) to improve our website performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Process your transactions and deliver the purchased digital services.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve our website features and user experience.

4. Data Protection and Third Parties

We implement strict security measures to safeguard your information. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We use authorized third-party services to ensure secure operations:

  • Paddle: Our payment processor (Merchant of Record). Paddle securely handles all payment information. We do not process or store your credit card details.
  • Google AdSense: We use Google advertising networks, which may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your visit to our site.
