Wyoming State Tax 2026: No Income Tax Guide

Jan 2026 StateTaxCalc Team 5 min read

Source Reference

Data in this article is sourced from official government releases:

  • Wyoming Dept. of Revenue

Good news for residents: Wyoming does not impose a state individual income tax. This can lead to significant savings compared to other states.

Key Updates & Brackets

While there is no income tax on wages, residents should be aware of other taxes:

  • Sales Tax: Wyoming relies heavily on sales tax revenue.
  • Property Tax: Property taxes may be higher to offset the lack of income tax.

Always check with the official department of revenue for the latest updates on other local taxes.
